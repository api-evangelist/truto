If you are building an AI agent that answers questions over a customer's Google Drive, Confluence, SharePoint, or Notion data, the single feature that will get you flagged in an enterprise security review is document-level permission enforcement. Not retrieval accuracy. Not latency. Permissions. You have built an impressive AI agent. It reasons correctly, plans multi-step workflows, and retrieves context accurately.
